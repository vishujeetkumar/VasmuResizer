        About Vasmu Resizer

        Vasmu Resizer is a free set of tools for everyday file jobs: make photos smaller, shrink PDFs for email, edit and sign documents, and convert between photo, PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint. It runs in your web browser, so there is nothing to install and no account to make.

        Questions people ask

        Are my files uploaded anywhere?

        No. Resizing, compressing, merging, splitting, editing, signing, converting and reading text from photos and scans (OCR) all happen inside your browser, so your files stay on your device. When you use text reading for the first time, your browser downloads a small language file, but your own files are never sent anywhere. The summarize and translate tools are coming soon.

        How do I resize a photo to a specific file size, like 200 KB?

        Open Resize photos, add your photo, choose JPG or WebP, type the size limit in KB under Target file size, and press Resize photos. The tool lowers quality and then dimensions until the file fits.

        Which photo formats can I use?

        You can open JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, TIFF and iPhone HEIC photos, and save as JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP or TIFF. Browsers cannot create new HEIC files, so HEIC is input only.

        How do I make a PDF smaller?

        Open Shrink files, add your PDF and pick Light, Balanced or Smallest. A lossless re-save is tried first; if that is not enough, pages are re-saved as images to reduce the size, so text in the smaller PDF may not be selectable. Word, Excel and PowerPoint files keep their text and get smaller pictures inside.

        Can I change text that is already in a PDF?

        Yes. Open PDF tools, choose Edit PDF, then Change existing text, click the text, type the new words and apply. The new text copies the old size and color, and the paper behind it is matched, which works best on plain backgrounds. You can also add new text, pictures and your signature. Only edit documents you own or have permission to change, and never alter official records to mislead anyone.

        Can I convert PDF to Word, Excel or PowerPoint?

        Yes. Open Convert files, add your PDF and pick Word, Excel or PowerPoint. Simple documents work best. Columns, fonts and page design are not kept, and PowerPoint slides are pictures of the pages.

        Can I get the text out of a photo or a scanned PDF?

        Yes. Open Convert files, add the photo or scanned PDF, and choose Word or Excel. For a scanned PDF you can also use OCR PDF in PDF tools to make it searchable. Printed text works best. Handwriting and blurry photos are hard to read, so check names and numbers in the result.

        Which other PDF tools are included?

        Merge, split, rotate, crop, organize pages, watermark, page numbers, protect with a password, unlock, repair, compare two PDFs, fill forms, scan to PDF, HTML to PDF, PDF to Markdown and redact. PMD to PDF opens old PageMaker 6 and 7 files right in your browser.

        BMP, TIFF and HEIC files can't be saved directly from this page, so they download inside a small ZIP.

        Large PDFs and many photos at once can take a moment, and very large files may be slow on phones. Older Word and PowerPoint files (.doc, .ppt) need to be re-saved as .docx or .pptx first.
