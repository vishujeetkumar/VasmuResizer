    Questions people ask

    Is it safe? Are my files uploaded?

    No upload happens. Your files are read and cleaned inside your own browser, and the cleaned copy is created on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

    Does removing metadata change the picture or the video?

    For JPG, PNG, WebP and video the picture data is not touched, so the quality stays exactly the same. Videos are not re-encoded. HEIC, TIFF and BMP photos are re-saved through the browser as a clean JPG or PNG.

    What is not removed?

    Faces, names or places that are visible inside a picture or video, comments and tracked changes inside Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, the names on PDF comments, and location tracks that sit inside the video stream itself (some action cameras and drones). Check the result before you share it.

    Why is my photo's rotation kept?

    Many phones store the rotation of a photo in its EXIF data. If everything were deleted, some photos would show sideways. Only the rotation value is kept, and it does not identify you.
