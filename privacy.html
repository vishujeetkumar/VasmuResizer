Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

The short version

Vasmu Resizer works inside your web browser. The photos, PDFs and documents you choose are processed on your own device. They are not uploaded to this website's server, and we do not keep copies of them.

Your files

When you resize, compress, merge, edit, sign, convert or read text from a file, all of that work is done by code running in your browser. Your files never leave your device through this website. When you close or refresh the page, the files and results that were in memory are gone.

What we do not collect

This website does not ask you to create an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address or phone number. It does not use advertising trackers or analytics tools.

Technical data and cookies from hosting

Like most websites, the company that hosts this website may record basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you open, and it may use cookies or similar technology for security (for example, to check that a visitor is a real browser). This is handled by the hosting provider under its own policies, not by the code of this tool.

Other services your browser contacts

To work, the page loads some resources from other providers. Your browser contacts them directly, and they can see technical details such as your IP address. They do not receive your files.

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) for the page fonts.
  • cdnjs (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) and jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) for the open-source code libraries that process files.
  • When you use text reading (OCR) for the first time, the OCR engine and a language file are downloaded from jsDelivr and from the Tesseract language data server (tessdata.projectnaptha.com). Your browser may keep these files in its cache so that it does not have to download them again.

Storage on your device

The tool does not save your files or personal details in your browser. The only thing it remembers is your light or dark theme choice, if you change it, so it can be used on your next visit. Your browser may also cache the code libraries and language files mentioned above to make later visits faster.

Donations

The Support page shows a UPI ID and QR code. When you choose to donate, the payment is made inside your own UPI app and bank. This website does not see, process or store any payment details. The "Pay with UPI app" button only opens your UPI app with the UPI ID and amount filled in.

Children

This website is a general file tool and is not aimed at collecting information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the way the website works changes, for example if a feature starts sending data to a server, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
